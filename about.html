About.

My name is Dimakatso Mokhele. "And I'm in love with being a masseuse." My love for massage comes from my passion for helping people, feel relaxed and cared for. I have a deep love for creating quiet, peaceful space where my clients relax, feel safe and reconnect with themselves.

At D. Matau Spa, you are never just a client lying on a massage table, you are a person with feeling, experience and a story that deserves to be respected. You are more than a booking or a massage session. You are someone who deserves kindness, understanding, genuine care and a peaceful space where you can feel valued heard and appreciated. My goal is to make every client feel comfortable, respected and special, because your well being matters to me beyond the massage itself.

I believe in the beauty of cleansing the aura, restoring positive energy and bringing balance and peace into every session. This what makes my passion for massage therapy so special and inspires me every day at D. Matau Spa.
