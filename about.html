MEET YOUR THERAPIST

My Story

My name is Dimakatso Mokhele, and I'm in love with being a masseuse. My love for massage comes from my passion for helping people feel relaxed and cared for. I have a deep love for creating a quiet, peaceful space where my clients can relax, feel safe and reconnect with themselves.

At D. Matau Spa, you are never just a client lying on a massage table. You are a person with feelings, experiences and a story that deserves to be respected.

You are more than a booking or a massage session. You deserve kindness, understanding, genuine care and a peaceful space where you can feel valued, heard and appreciated.

My goal is to make every client feel comfortable, respected and special, because your well-being matters to me beyond the massage itself.

OUR PHILOSOPHY

Restoring Balance and Peace

I believe in the beauty of cleansing the aura, restoring positive energy and bringing balance and peace into every session. This is what makes my passion for massage therapy so special and inspires me every day at D. Matau Spa.

WHY CHOOSE US

Your Spa Experience, Your Space

We bring relaxation, comfort and self-care directly to you in the comfort of your own home or hotel.

Our mobile spa service allows you to enjoy a peaceful spa experience without the stress of travelling. We specialise in home spa treatments, including relaxing massages and body scrubbing, designed to help you unwind, refresh your skin and release the stress of everyday life.

Choose D. Matau Spa for a personalised experience where your comfort and well-being come first.

Let us bring the spa experience to your doorstep and help you make time for yourself.
