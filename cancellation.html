Cancelation Policy

  1. Deposits

    Deposits may be required to secure your booking and may be non-refundable. For late cancellations, provided this is communicated clearly in advance and complies with applicable law.

  2. 24-Hour Notice

    Please cancel or reschedule via 084 655 8531 whatsapp for your appointment at least 24 hours in advance.

  3. No-show

    Clients who miss their appointments without notifying us may be required to pay a fee before booking again.

  4. Late Arrivals

    Please arrive 5-10 minutes early. Late arrivals may result in a shorter sessions.

Spa Cancellations:

If we need to cancel your appointment, we will notify you as soon as possible and offer to reschedule or refund any payment for services not provided.
