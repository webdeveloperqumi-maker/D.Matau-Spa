IMPORTANT BOOKING INFORMATION

Cancellation Policy

We understand that plans can change. Please read our policy before arranging your appointment.

1. Deposits

Deposits may be required to secure your booking and may be non-refundable, subject to applicable law and the circumstances of the cancellation. Any deposit requirements will be communicated clearly in advance.

2. 24-Hour Notice

Please cancel or reschedule your appointment via WhatsApp on 084 655 8531 at least 24 hours in advance.

3. No-Shows

Clients who miss their appointments without notifying the spa may be required to pay a fee before booking again. Any applicable fee will be communicated in advance.

4. Late Arrivals

Please arrive or be ready for your appointment 5–10 minutes early. Late arrivals may result in a shorter session to avoid affecting other bookings.

5. Spa Cancellations

If we need to cancel your appointment, we will notify you as soon as possible and offer to reschedule or refund any payment for services not provided, as applicable.
